# Source Verification Skill

When this skill is activated, critically assess evidence, audit factual claims, and eliminate inaccuracies:

1. **Multi-Source Triangulation**: Corroborate all key factual claims, numbers, and dates across at least two independent, high-reputation sources.
2. **Domain Credibility Assessment**: Assess institutional legitimacy (.edu, .gov, peer-reviewed journals, official documentation) versus unvetted opinion blogs or forums.
3. **Temporal Validity Checks**: Verify publication and last-updated timestamps to ensure information has not been superseded by newer releases or policy updates.
4. **Contextual Fidelity**: Inspect original surrounding context to ensure quoted statistics, benchmarks, and statements are not taken out of context or distorted.
5. **Conflict Identification**: Explicitly highlight contradictions between reputable sources, assessing potential methodological differences or underlying biases.
6. **Provenance Attribution**: Maintain an auditable verification trail with explicit confidence scores and direct reference links for each claim.
